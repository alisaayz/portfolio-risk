## Priorities to test

These are hypotheses from the Version 1 interface inspection. Reorder them using participant observations.

1. **Exposure and target confusion.** Test whether people mistake “Meets target” for meeting the whole decision brief, and whether they confuse historical volatility with a future loss limit. Give any observed critical misunderstanding priority.
2. **Hidden controls and setup friction.** Test whether participants can find the manual reference, locks, and candidate screen without assistance. Record where their progress stops.
3. **Window sensitivity.** Test whether participants recognize that the 63- and 252-session results use different historical evidence and that the case requires 252 sessions.

After the pilot, replace the pending cells and log each observed issue with the affected task, number of participants, consequence, and proposed Version 2 change.
