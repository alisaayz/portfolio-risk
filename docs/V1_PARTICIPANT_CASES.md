# riskitnot decision tasks

Use the demonstration portfolio in the local dashboard. Each case starts with AAPL at 30%, SPY at 50%, and BND at 20%. Reset the demo between cases. Use the saved November 3, 2026 snapshot. Make a decision for the fictional investor in each case.

## Case A. Reduce risk while keeping a holding fixed

You want to lower the portfolio's estimated historical volatility to **10.60% or less** over **252 trading sessions**. Keep AAPL at **30%**, use the same three holdings, and move at most **10% of total portfolio value**.

Decide whether you would keep the current allocation or compare a reweighted one. Show the evidence you used. Explain the estimated effect on volatility and historical drawdown, and say what the estimate tells you about a future loss.

## Case B. Reduce risk while preserving exposure

You are considering moving **10% of total portfolio value out of AAPL** into a single new holding. The replacement must be an **equity ETF** so the portfolio keeps its 80% stock and 20% bond mix. Keep SPY and BND at their original weights. Your target is **10.00% historical volatility or less** over **252 trading sessions**.

Would you compare a replacement or keep the current portfolio for now? Explain how you checked that your choice met both the exposure rule and the volatility target.

After making that decision, check the same setup using **63 trading sessions**. What changed, and how would it affect your confidence in the decision?
